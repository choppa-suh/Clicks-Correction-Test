# Click Correction

Forgives clicks that come in a little too early.

Open the pause menu and press the green **CC** button to turn the correction on
or off and to choose how many frames of leeway you want (1 to 30, default 4).

When you click while still in the air in a mode that needs the ground (cube,
ball, robot, spider), the click is remembered for a few frames. If you land
inside that window, the click is applied for you, so the jump or gravity flip
still happens.

All options are also available in the mod settings page.
